\textbf{Exact orthogonalization.} The run with $p=0$ computed by SVD (seed 10 only) behaves like standard Muon in the same seed. It reaches error 0.05 in 450 steps against 500. Its probe score is 0.12, 0.21 and 0.35 at errors 0.05, 0.04 and 0.03, against 0.16, 0.21 and 0.32 for Muon and 0.06, 0.14 and 0.23 for AdamW. Its bounded score is 0.38, 0.48 and 0.55, against 0.41, 0.45 and 0.53 (AdamW: 0.38, 0.43, 0.51). Its query-shift ratios to AdamW are within 10\% of standard Muon's at every shift at errors 0.05 and 0.04.
