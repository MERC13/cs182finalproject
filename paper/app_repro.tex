Code, job files, training logs and checkpoints are at \url{https://github.com/MERC13/cs182finalproject}. The scripts in \texttt{scripts/} compute every figure and number in this paper from the committed logs (the README lists which script produces what), \texttt{scripts/run\_queue.py} retrains any run, and \texttt{latexmk -pdf main.tex} in \texttt{paper/} builds this PDF. All runs used CPUs, about 80 CPU-hours in total at Garg scale.
