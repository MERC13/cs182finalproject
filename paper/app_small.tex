\textbf{Linear attention.} The model of Section~3.2 ($d = 8$, $N = 32$, 8 heads with rank-one keys and queries, weights initialized from $\mathcal N(0, 10^{-4})$) is trained on batches of 1,024 fresh prompts with $\lambda_j \propto 1/j^2$. In the principal basis, with query covariance $\Lambda_q$ and $A = \Gamma\Lambda_q\Gamma^\top$, its loss is
\begin{equation*}
L = \tfrac1d\Big[\tfrac{N+1}{N}\,\mathrm{tr}(\Lambda A\Lambda) + \tfrac1N\,\mathrm{tr}(A\Lambda)\,\mathrm{tr}\,\Lambda - 2\,\mathrm{tr}(\Lambda\Gamma\Lambda_q) + \mathrm{tr}\,\Lambda_q\Big],
\end{equation*}
and $L^\star = 1 - \frac1d\sum_j \lambda_j\rho_j^\star$. Both are computed exactly from $\Gamma$. The learning-rate sweeps ran 4,000 steps and logged every 10 steps. For the results in this paper the selected runs were repeated with every step logged and extended to 6,000 steps, so that all three SGD seeds reach excess loss 0.1; training is deterministic and the rate constant, so the first 4,000 steps reproduce the original runs exactly. Logging every step matters because every arm but SGD crosses the window from excess loss 0.5 to 0.1 in about 20--75 steps. SGD here is plain gradient descent without momentum, as in \citet{zhang2025training}; Adam has no weight decay. Muon and the knob arms update the stacked key and query matrices, with Adam at the same learning rate on the head scalars $v_h$. Each arm's learning rate is selected on seed 0 from a six-point grid by mean loss over training, and seeds 1--2 are run at that rate (Table~\ref{tab:smallhp}). For the PCR-versus-ridge comparison we clip the gain profile to $g_j = \min(\max(\rho_j/\rho_j^\star, 0), 1.2)$ and compare it with a PCR step $\mathbf 1[j \le m]$ ($m = 0,\dots,d$) and a ridge curve $\lambda_j/(\lambda_j + a)$ (200 log-spaced $a \in [10^{-3}, 10^2]$): $R_{\rm PCR} = \min_m \sum_j (g_j - \mathbf 1[j\le m])^2$ and $R_{\rm ridge} = \min_a \sum_j (g_j - \lambda_j/(\lambda_j+a))^2$. Each residual is averaged over 8 log-spaced excess-loss levels from 0.5 to 0.1, which every run reaches (profiles interpolated linearly between consecutive steps), and we report $R_{\rm PCR}/R_{\rm ridge}$ per seed. The ``mean gain on directions 3--8'' in Table~\ref{tab:summary} is the mean over $j = 3,\dots,8$ of $\rho_j/\rho_j^\star$ at excess loss 0.1, from the median profile over seeds. Query shifts use the same $\Lambda^{1-2t}$ family, evaluated exactly from $\Gamma$ logged every step around the matched levels. At excess loss 0.1 (median over seeds), standard Muon's error relative to Adam's is 1.20 and 1.15 for the two most common-ward shifts ($t=-1, -0.5$) and 0.95 and 0.97 for the two most rare-ward ones ($t=0.5, 1$). For exact orthogonalization these are 0.90, 0.97, 0.94 and 0.93; for $p=1$ and SGD 0.88, 0.91, 1.03 and 1.01; for $p = 0.1$ 0.97, 0.99, 0.97 and 0.96. At excess loss 0.05 standard Muon's ratios are 0.99, 1.01, 0.97 and 0.97. SGD never reaches excess loss 0.05.

\textbf{Smaller GPT-2.} 4 layers, 4 heads, width 128, $d = 8$, 32 examples, batch 64, hidden initialization 0.044, the token layout of Section~3.2 and $\lambda_j \propto 1/j^2$; seeds 10 and 11 (Table~\ref{tab:smallhp}). The knob arms ($p = 1, 0.25, 0$; seed 10) all use Muon's rate 0.007, so $p=1$ is untuned and about seven times slower to error 0.03. Read-outs are taken at the first evaluation at or below each level (every 25 steps): the fraction of the weakest direction learned ($f_8$; mean of seeds 10--11) and the probe score $\bar f$ over directions 3--8 at error 0.03; the PCR/ridge comparison at errors 0.1 and 0.05. The query shift (seed 10 only) is read at errors 0.05, 0.03 and 0.01 of the relative error $\sum(\hat y - y)^2/\sum y^2$ on its 256 prompts. All probe scores in this model are positive at these levels, so the bounded and raw scores agree.

\textbf{Interpretation caveat.} The linear-attention profile measures the learned gain $\rho_j/\rho^\star_j$ directly, while the GPT-2 measurements infer it from predictions. The PCR-versus-ridge statistics in the two settings are therefore different measurements of the same idea and should not be compared numerically.
